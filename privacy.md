# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how GTA6 Clip Factory handles information when users connect third-party platforms.

## 2. Information We Access
When users connect their accounts, the application may access basic account information and permissions needed for its features, such as uploading videos.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information is used only to provide application features, manage account connections, and upload content at the user's request.

## 4. Data Storage
Authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's device using encryption. Users should keep their device secure.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The application may use official APIs from YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, and Kick. Their own privacy policies apply to information processed by those services.

## 6. Data Sharing
The application does not sell users' personal information.

## 7. Data Deletion
Users can disconnect their accounts and delete locally stored application data.

## 8. Contact
For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository.
